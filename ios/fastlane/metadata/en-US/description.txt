GuzoMy connects people who need items delivered with verified travelers who are already flying that route. Senders get their packages where they need to go, and travelers put their spare luggage space to use.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose your mode: switch between Sender and Carrier at any time
• Travelers post an offer with their flight, the items they can carry, and a price per item
• Senders post a request for the items they need delivered, or match directly with a traveler's offer
• Travelers send proposals on open requests, and senders pick the one that fits
• Once matched, chat in the app to arrange pickup, delivery, and the handoff

BUILT ON TRUST
• Identity verification: members verify a government-issued ID before they create offers, requests, or proposals
• Verified badges on profiles, so you know who you're dealing with
• Photo confirmation at pickup starts a tracked delivery
• Third-party receivers: send to a family member or friend, with their details and ID

FOR SENDERS
• Find travelers flying from your city to where your package needs to go
• Search by airport, city, or country
• Compare prices, discounts, payment options, and meetup points
• Track each delivery from pickup to drop-off

FOR TRAVELERS (CARRIERS)
• Turn unused luggage space into extra income on trips you're already taking
• Set your own price per item, currency, and discounts
• Browse open requests along your route and send proposals
• Manage all your offers and deliveries in one place

STAY IN THE LOOP
• Real-time messaging with your match
• Push notifications for new proposals, matches, and delivery updates
• Available in English, Amharic, French, and Arabic

GuzoMy makes sending to friends, family, and customers abroad more personal, affordable, and fast, whether you're sending gifts home, documents, or everyday essentials.

Travelers are responsible for following airline rules and customs regulations in the countries they travel between. Do not send prohibited, dangerous, or illegal items.
